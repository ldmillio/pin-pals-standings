Calculation Explanation

All-play – Each week a team's score is compared with the other 11 teams. A higher score is a win and a lower score is a loss. The record in the table adds those up.

Power ranking – The PR score column is this number, and a higher score is better. It is 50% all-play win rate, 31% lineup edge, and 19% rest-of-season points. Eight of the 13 regular-season weeks are complete, so lineup edge gets 8/13 of the other half and rest-of-season points get 5/13. Those points are Sleeper projections for weeks 9–13, before the playoffs, for the best lineup from starters and bench. Bye weeks in that span count as zero. These are Sleeper's standard PPR projections, so this league's tight end bonus and rushing first downs are not included.

Start/sit – For each week, the best lineup is the highest-scoring group that roster could have started, using the points each player scored. The column is points scored over weeks 1–8 divided by the points from those best lineups.

Draft round – For each week, this is the average draft round of the skill players who started. Kickers and defenses are left out. A player who was not drafted counts as round 23. The column is the average of those weekly averages for weeks 1–8.

Age – Same starters as the draft round column. Each player's age is their age on that week's game date. The column is the average of those weekly averages for weeks 1–8.

Playoff % – The share of 10,000 simulated seasons in which the team finishes in the top 6. The record and points through week 8 stay as they are. For each week from 9 through 13, a team is centered on its best projected lineup from starters and bench, and the opponent is centered on theirs. A bye counts as zero. The simulated score is drawn around that center using the team's scoring spread from weeks 1–8. Wins come first, then points for. These centers are Sleeper's standard PPR projections, so this league's tight end bonus and rushing first downs are not included.

Luckiest team – Expected wins are all-play wins divided by 11. Luck is actual wins minus expected wins. The luckiest team is the most wins above that number.

Unluckiest team – Same calculation. The unluckiest team is the most wins below that number.

Wall of Shame – Started a player who was labeled out prior to the game start, or whose team was on a bye.
